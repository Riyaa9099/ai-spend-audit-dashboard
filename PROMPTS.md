# Prompts

## Audit Summary Prompt

The audit summary explains:
- total tools used
- estimated monthly savings
- estimated yearly savings
- optimization suggestions


## Why I Wrote Prompts This Way

I wanted concise summaries that are easy for startup teams and founders to understand quickly.

The prompts focus on:
- savings
- optimization
- clarity
- readability



## What Didn’t Work

Initially, I tried generating long audit summaries, but they became repetitive and hard to read.

I also experimented with technical explanations, but simpler language worked better for users.

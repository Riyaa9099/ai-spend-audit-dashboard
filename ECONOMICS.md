# Economics

## Lead Value Estimate

If Credex converts one startup customer into a financial services client, the estimated value could range between $500–$3000 depending on the financial product used.


## Customer Acquisition Cost

### Organic Social
- Estimated CAC: $5–$15
- Mainly time investment

### Founder Communities
- Estimated CAC: $10–$25
- Through direct outreach and networking

### Product Hunt
- Estimated CAC: $20–$40
- Depends on visibility and engagement



## Conversion Funnel

Example funnel:
- 1000 visitors
- 150 audits completed
- 25 consultation bookings
- 5 paying customers

This creates roughly:
- 15% audit conversion
- 16% consultation conversion
- 20% customer conversion


## Path to Profitability

The tool becomes profitable if:
- CAC stays under $40
- Each converted customer generates at least $500 revenue
- Conversion rates remain stable


## Path to $1M ARR

To reach $1M ARR in 18 months:
- 3000+ startups using the tool monthly
- 3–5% converting into paid financial services
- Strong organic growth through founder communities and referrals

The most important factor would be building trust and becoming a standard AI spending optimization tool for startups.

# User Interviews

## Interview 1

### Person
A.S. — Student founder building an AI productivity startup

### Quotes
- “We keep subscribing to AI tools without tracking usage.”
- “Most teams don’t realize how much AI costs add up monthly.”
- “A simple dashboard would actually help.”

### Most Surprising Thing
They cared more about visibility than cost savings initially.

### Design Changes
Added charts and summary sections for quick visibility.



## Interview 2

### Person
R.K. — Freelance developer using multiple AI coding tools

### Quotes
- “I use ChatGPT, Cursor, and Copilot together.”
- “I forget which subscriptions are worth paying for.”
- “Recommendations are more useful than raw spending numbers.”

### Most Surprising Thing
They wanted optimization suggestions automatically.

### Design Changes
Improved recommendation engine visibility.


## Interview 3

### Person
N.M. — College student working on side projects

### Quotes
- “I switch between free and paid AI plans often.”
- “Currency support matters because pricing feels different in INR.”
- “Downloadable reports are useful for tracking.”

### Most Surprising Thing
Currency selection was more important than expected.

### Design Changes
Added dynamic currency selector.

# Go To Market Strategy

## Target User

The ideal target user is a startup founder or operations lead at an early-stage SaaS company with 5–50 employees. These teams often subscribe to multiple AI tools quickly without monitoring spending efficiency.



## What They Search Before Using This Tool

Users typically search:
- “best AI tools for startups”
- “AI SaaS stack”
- “reduce AI subscription costs”
- “ChatGPT vs Claude pricing”
- “AI tools for productivity”



## Where They Hang Out Online

- Indie Hackers
- Product Hunt
- Reddit communities like r/SaaS and r/startups
- Founder Twitter/X communities
- Startup Discord groups



## First 100 Users

I would:
- Share the tool on Product Hunt
- Post demo videos on LinkedIn and Twitter/X
- Reach out to startup founders directly
- Share in founder communities and Discords
- Use college startup networks and hackathon groups



## Unfair Distribution Advantage

The advantage is targeting startup founders early before they scale their AI spending inefficiently. Credex could integrate financial recommendations directly into the audit flow.



## Week 1 Traction Goals

- 100 completed audits
- 20 report downloads
- 10 consultation signups
- 5 startup referrals
